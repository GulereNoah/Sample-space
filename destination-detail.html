    Murchison Falls National Park

    Northwestern Uganda

    3,840 km² Protected Area 76 Mammal Species 450+ Birds From $620

    Destination Overview

    Murchison Falls National Park is Uganda's largest and most dramatic savannah reserve, defined by the explosive power of the Nile as it forces through an 8-meter gorge before plunging 45 meters below.

    This is classic East African safari terrain, open plains, acacia woodland, and riverine ecosystems supporting lion, leopard, elephant, giraffe, buffalo, and one of Africa's densest hippo populations.

    Refined Experiences

    • Structured game drives with expert trackers
    • Private Nile boat expeditions to the Devil's Cauldron
    • Top-of-the-falls guided hikes
    • Optional chimpanzee trekking in Budongo Forest
    Traveler Profile

    The park suits both luxury lodge stays and high-end photographic safaris. It is a cornerstone of Simbiya's big game circuits.

    Best Season: June-September & December-February.

    Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

    Southwestern Uganda

    UNESCO World Heritage Site ~460 Mountain Gorillas 120 Mammals From $1,550

    Destination Overview

    Bwindi is not simply a destination it is a rare ecological sanctuary holding nearly half of the world’s remaining mountain gorillas. The forest is ancient, biodiverse, and intensely immersive.

    Gorilla trekking here is conducted under strict conservation protocols, guided by professional rangers and trackers trained in primate behavior and safety.

    Refined Experiences

    • Private birding expeditions (350+ species)
    • Forest waterfall trails
    • Batwa cultural heritage experiences
    • High-end eco-lodge stays overlooking mist-covered valleys
    Traveler Profile

    Bwindi is the cornerstone of Simbiya's primate safaris and a defining experience for international clientele seeking rarity and depth.

    Best Season: June-August & December-February.

    Kibale Forest National Park

    Western Uganda

    13 Primate Species 1,500+ Chimpanzees 795 km² Forest From $1680 pp

    Destination Overview

    Kibale is East Africa's primate capital, offering some of the highest chimpanzee tracking success rates in Africa. This biodiverse tropical forest is home to 13 species of primates.

    Refined Experiences

    • Chimpanzee habituation and tracking
    • Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary walks
    • Night forest walks for nocturnal primates
    • Golden monkey and red colobus sightings
    Traveler Profile

    Experiences here are intimate, educational, and conservation-driven. Simbiya designs Kibale extensions as part of refined primate circuits.

    Best Season: June-September & December-February.
